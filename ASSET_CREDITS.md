# Asset packs

The game uses free packs through a small runtime asset registry.

| Pack | Use | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quaternius Universal Base Characters | rigged humanoid player/NPCs | CC0 | https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters |
| Quaternius Universal Animation Library | shared humanoid animation clips | CC0 | https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library |
| Quaternius Downtown City MegaKit | modular city buildings | CC0 | https://quaternius.itch.io/downtown-city-megakit |
| Gobkit Free Minions | additional rigged/animated NPC variants | CC0 | https://gobkit.itch.io/gobkit-free-minions |

Runtime references are declared in `src/assets/AssetPackRegistry.js`.

The character/building GLB mirrors are used as web-delivery sources; the original packs remain the authoritative source and license reference.
